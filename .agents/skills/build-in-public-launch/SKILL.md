# Build in Public and Launch

## Goal
Get Vid-Visual seen by the right people with honest, specific posts, and turn attention into signups.

## Lenses (ideas only)
- Pieter Levels: ship in public, share real numbers and real progress.
- Daniel Vassallo, Small Bets: many small public experiments beat one big secret launch.
- Arvid Kahl, Zero to Sold: build an audience and learn from it before and during the product.

## Launch checklist (T minus 14 days to T plus 7 days)
- Landing page ready: clear headline, demo, pricing, FAQ, working signup.
- 5 strong example outputs that look professional.
- 30 to 60 second demo video with captions.
- Analytics, error monitoring, and support email working.
- Share images (Open Graph) checked.
- Free plan tested end to end by 3 people who are not me.
- Launch posts drafted. Replies planned for the first 2 hours.

## Templates
- X thread: hook with the result (a real before and after), the problem, how it works in 3 steps, one honest lesson from building, the call to action. Real screenshots only.
- Weekly update: shipped / one real metric / one lesson / one ask.
- Changelog entry: date, what changed, why it matters to the user.
- Product Hunt: tagline, first comment from the maker, gallery images, a plan to answer every comment. Follow the platform rules. Never ask for upvotes in manipulative ways.

## Community rules
Read each community's rules before posting. Share value first, link second. No copy-paste spam. Disclose that I am the maker.

## Honesty rules
Only true numbers. Never invent users, revenue, or testimonials. Disclose partnerships and cross-promotions clearly.

## Output
A day-by-day calendar table: Day / Channel / Post draft / Asset needed / Goal metric.
