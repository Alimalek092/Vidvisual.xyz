# Growth, Activation, and Product-Led Growth

## Goal
Get more new users to their first result they are proud of, then keep them and convert the right ones to paid, without dark patterns.

## Lenses
- Dave McClure, AARRR: Acquisition, Activation, Retention, Revenue, Referral.
- Sean Ellis: pick a North Star metric, run small experiments, prioritize with ICE.
- Andrew Chen, The Cold Start Problem: loops beat funnels. Design the product so using it brings in the next user.
- Elena Verna and Wes Bush: product-led growth. The product itself acquires, converts, and retains.

## Core definitions (propose, then confirm with real data)
- Aha moment: the user exports or shares a visual or video they are proud of.
- North Star candidate: weekly users who complete an export.
- Time to first value target: under 3 minutes from landing to first great preview. Treat as a target to test.

## Playbook
1. Activation: let people try before signup if possible. Ask at most 1 or 2 questions. Start from templates, not a blank page. Show a progress checklist.
2. Retention: templates they can reuse, saved projects, a weekly prompt idea, an email for unfinished projects.
3. Loops: free-tier exports carry a small "Made with Vid-Visual" mark that links back. Public gallery pages that are good enough to rank (see seo-content-engine). Referral credits for both sides.
4. Conversion: upgrade at the moment of need, show what they already created and what they unlock, display a usage meter, send a 3-email sequence (welcome, first win, upgrade reminder).
5. Experiments: write the hypothesis, the one metric, the sample size needed, and the stop rule. One experiment at a time.

## Honesty about numbers
- With small traffic, A/B tests mean nothing. Use qualitative feedback and before/after comparisons, and say so.
- Benchmarks from blogs vary a lot. Quote them as "commonly cited" and measure our own.

## Ethics
No fake scarcity, fake countdowns, fake "X people are viewing", hidden cancel buttons, or pre-ticked upsells. Growth must survive users reading about how it works.

## Output
Funnel map with current numbers (or "unknown, add tracking"), the biggest drop-off, three ICE-scored experiments, and the first one specified fully.
